Environmental scan of employment after spinal cord injury

Australia · 2016–2026

This work was conducted by the Aus-InSCI Translation Project team during March and July 2026. The main focus of this environmental scan is to capture the employment environment for people with spinal cord injury (SCI) in Australia.

Systematic review of published grey literature was undertaken using keywords related to employment outcomes following SCI in Australia. The published literature searches were conducted on 26 March, and grey literature searches between 23 and 30 March. The search focused on the past 10 years, with the exception of legislation. Input from relevant stakeholders was collected via survey and meetings with an expert working group between April and August. Identified resources were then assessed against predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria. AI tools were used to generate summaries of the resources, with all outputs reviewed and verified by the research team.

For more information about the environmental scan, please contact the Aus-InSCI Translation Project team at aus-insci_translationproject@health.qld.gov.au.
